## 2024-09-15 - Replace O(N) scroll listener with IntersectionObserver
**Learning:** This codebase had a global `window.addEventListener('scroll')` that iterated over all `.scroll-reveal` elements and called `getBoundingClientRect()` on every scroll tick. Even worse, there were zero elements with this class in the HTML, meaning the app was doing O(N) querying (`querySelectorAll`) and causing layout thrashing on every scroll for absolutely no visual benefit.
**Action:** When implementing scroll-based reveal animations, always prefer `IntersectionObserver` over binding to the `scroll` event. Additionally, guard the initialization block with a check `if (elements.length > 0)` to prevent the observer from being created unnecessarily. Call `observer.unobserve()` once the animation triggers to avoid continuous monitoring.
